Style board · Chalk + Plush

Soft as Ollie. Drawn in chalk.

Sidewalk chalk colors, soft squishy shapes like a plush toy, and stickers as the reward. One look for every age; the kid version turns the same pieces up.

Soft like a plush toy

Pillowy buttons with soft glows, round everything, no hard edges or block shadows. It should feel like Ollie looks.

✏️

Drawn in chalk

The path is a hand-drawn chalk line on the sidewalk. Dashed, a little wobbly, like a kid drew it.

⭐

Stickers are the reward

Every stop and badge is a die-cut sticker: white edge, slight tilt, a little shadow. Collecting them is the game.
